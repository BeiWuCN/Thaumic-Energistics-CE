# Thaumic Energistics: CE — Mod Description

## For Modrinth

Thaumic Energistics: CE Bridges Thaumaturge's essentia into Applied Energistics 2 ME networks. Essentia lives on the network, moves across it, and is autocrafted by it.

This mod is a rewrite of the original Thaumic Energistics, which stopped at 1.12.2, built for 1.21.1 on top of Thaumaturge's API. It is released under the MIT license.

### Storage

ME Essentia Storage Components come in five tiers: 1k, 4k, 16k, 64k, and creative. They go into ME Drives and ME Chests. The Essentia Cell Workbench partitions a storage component. The Essentia Terminal lists every essentia type and amount on the network.

### Essentia machines

The Distillation Encoder encodes distillation recipes into patterns. The Alchemy Provider feeds essentia into a crucible, and the Alchemy Wireless Receiver reaches it without a cable; the Wireless Connector pairs the two. The Essentia Vibration Chamber turns essentia into energy. The Infusion Provider feeds essentia into an infusion altar and reports the risk of each infusion. The Occult Monitor reads the aura and the aspect field around it. The Essentia Level Emitter emits a redstone signal on an essentia threshold.

### Arcane crafting

The Arcane Assembly Chamber autocrafts from patterns, taking ingredients from the ME network and returning products to it. An Arcane Pattern makes a recipe available as a stored pattern, and the Knowledge Core holds those patterns. The Arcane Crafting Terminal and the Wireless Arcane Crafting Terminal fold arcane crafting into the ME network's interface. Both settle the vis cost of a craft through the network.

### Vis and flux

The Vis Relay Interface stores vis on the network and takes it back out. The Flux Transfer Interface converts between essentia and flux, and collects flux off the network.

### Wireless and extras

The Wireless Essentia Terminal, Wireless Arcane Crafting Terminal, and Wireless Connector let terminals work off the cable. The Golem Wireless Backpack puts golems on the network. The Gacha Box and the Gacha Box Aggregator pay out observation and theory for research points. The Focus of the AE Wrench is a focus for the AE2 wrench, and it repairs network connections in place.

### Requirements

- Minecraft 1.21.1
- NeoForge 21.1.250 or newer
- Applied Energistics 2 19.2.x
- Thaumaturge 0.4.4 or newer

JEI, Jade, Curios, and GuideME are optional. With them installed the mod shows recipes, block information, and guide pages. It runs without them.
