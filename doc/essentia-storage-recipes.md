# 源质存储单元配方

1k、4k、16k、64k 四级完整单元配方的三枚底部锭统一使用神秘锭，与空外壳一致；铁锭不再匹配。

各级新增普通工作台无序配方：一件空存储外壳＋一件对应容量存储元件，产出一个该容量源质存储单元。输入无需相邻或固定位置。

## essentia_cell_1k.json

迁移前配方说明（原文，已不代表当前材料）：

```text
The 1.12.2 build's essentia_cell_1k, shape and materials both: quartz glass in the top corners, a redstone frame, the 1k component in the middle and an iron base. The 1.12.2 build took plain quartz glass and the ingotIron ore dictionary; this build takes its own glass tag (AE2 quartz glass or Thaumaturge warded glass) and a plain iron ingot, which is how the sibling storage_casing is written.
```
