# Essentia Vibration Chamber

Distilled essentia still holds energy. Rather than feed more fuel into the vibration chamber, I altered its inlet to burn essentia directly.

After the first point was consumed, the energy buffer registered a charge and the network began drawing from it. Another aspect gave a different burn time and output. They cannot all be treated as the same fuel.<PAGE>Pipes and an essentia export bus can both replenish the supply. I kept three readings on the display: essentia remaining, burn progress, and stored energy. They were useful for comparing the trials.

When the buffer filled, the burn stopped and the inlet's suction withdrew. It resumed only after the network drew more energy. At least it should not consume an entire jar when there is no demand.
