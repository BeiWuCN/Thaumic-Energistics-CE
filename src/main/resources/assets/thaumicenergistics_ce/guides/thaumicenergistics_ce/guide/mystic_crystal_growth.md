# Crystal Cultivation

## Step 1

AE2 budding certus quartz already has a stable growth structure. Infuse it directly with thaumium or void metal to grow thaumic crystals, preserving its corresponding quality. Chipped budding quartz becomes a chipped budding thaumic crystal, and damaged budding quartz becomes a damaged budding thaumic crystal. No amethyst block is needed.

## Step 2

Buds grow through small, medium and large stages into a mature cluster. A mature cluster yields four thaumic crystals with a suitable pickaxe; Fortune increases the yield, while Silk Touch preserves the bud or cluster. Matrices produce crystals, never metal. Each successful growth step has a 1/12 chance to degrade a non-flawless matrix. A damaged budding thaumic crystal becomes an thaumic crystal block if it degrades again, so restore it by infusion before then. Four crystals pack into a reversible storage block.
