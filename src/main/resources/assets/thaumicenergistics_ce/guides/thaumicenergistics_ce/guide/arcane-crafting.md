# Arcane Crafting

The <ItemLink id="thaumicenergistics_ce:arcane_crafting_terminal" /> gives you an arcane crafting grid and access to ME item storage from the same screen. Recipes still require the appropriate research and payment.

## Payment

Place the wand and primal crystals in their dedicated slots. The toolbar button chooses whether to spend crystals or wand Vis first. The terminal remembers your preference. Check the cost beside the result if you cannot craft.

Crafting spends ingredients in three tiers to preserve the layout you arranged: surplus stock beyond one grid's worth in the crafting grid and crystal slots is spent first; once that surplus is gone and only one template portion remains, ME storage supplies the ingredients while the grid and crystal slots stay untouched, so you can click the result repeatedly; only when the network holds less than one grid's worth does it draw on that last local portion, mixing network and local stock. A crystal shortfall is likewise drawn from ME storage in crystal-first mode, or when crystals are the only viable payment.

JEI can transfer an arcane recipe and supplement the required crystals from ME storage. To clear the grid, use the upper button beside the result to return ingredients to ME or the lower one to return them to your inventory. Both buttons return the grid ingredients and crystals. Anything that does not fit stays in its slot.
