# Vis Connection Card

## Step 1

The placed arcane terminal draws aura directly from its own chunk, while the wand still needs primal vis.

A Basic Card can record a Vis Relay Interface output, allowing the terminal to recharge the wand in its slot.

## Step 2

Sneak-use the §lVis Connection Card§r on a Vis Relay Interface output, then install the card in the terminal. A loaded, online output supplies primal vis to recharge the wand.

The card does not supply chunk aura. Crafting still consumes environmental VIS.
