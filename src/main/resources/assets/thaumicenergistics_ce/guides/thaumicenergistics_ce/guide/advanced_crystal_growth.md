# Advanced Crystal Cultivation

## Step 1

Infuse flawless budding quartz directly into a flawless budding thaumic crystal, or flawed budding quartz into a flawed budding thaumic crystal. These advanced recipes use void metal and a thaumic processor. Every quality produces the same thaumic crystal.

## Step 2

Infuse the corresponding budding certus quartz into a void matrix for faster growth. Flawless matrices never degrade from growth. Flawed, chipped and damaged budding crystals each have a 1/12 chance to degrade per successful growth step, ultimately becoming a thaumic crystal block. Every growing state produces the same crystal. Growth attempts per random tick have probabilities of 1/2, 1/3, 1/5 and 1/8 respectively. Reinfuse a flawed matrix with void metal and a processor to restore it.
