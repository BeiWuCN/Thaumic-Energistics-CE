# Thaumic Processing

## Step 1

A printed crystal circuit becomes a controller when combined with printed silicon and redstone. It does not require another completed AE2 processor.

## Step 2

Put a thaumic circuit board in the top slot, printed silicon in the bottom slot and redstone in the middle slot of an AE2 Inscriber. All three inputs are consumed to make one thaumic processor. No press or VIS is needed.

Infusion providers, arcane assemblers, wireless terminals and high-density storage use it.
