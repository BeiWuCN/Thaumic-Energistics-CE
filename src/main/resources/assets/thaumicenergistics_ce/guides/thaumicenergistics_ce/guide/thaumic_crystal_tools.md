# Thaumic Crystal Tools

## Step 1

The edge of my Fluix pickaxe has chipped again. I kept the piece this time: a little vis drew its cracks together, though the handle remained quite ordinary.

A smithing template should let me reinforce the existing tool without disturbing its enchantments. The new crystal need not replace every part of it.

## Step 2

The template I brought back from the ruins has finally earned its place on the bench. I tried it on my old Fluix pickaxe, forging a whole block of thaumic crystal into the head. Its enchantments survived. So did the name cut into the handle.

The new pick makes quicker work of stone, though it still wears down. I left it on the recharge pedestal; when I returned, the notch in its tip had closed. I shall have to get into the habit of putting it back. Carrying it around does nothing for the damage.

The template does not survive forging, so I must keep one aside to copy. Seven blocks of thaumic crystal and a thaumium ingot make two templates from one. Perhaps my next visit to the ruins can involve something besides brushing sand and gravel.

The wrench and cutting knife need less fuss. Their old quartz patterns work as they are, with thaumic crystal in place of Certus Quartz.
