# Crystal Circuits

## Step 1

An existing Inscriber press already has precise grooves. I lowered one into the crucible, letting cognition and order guide the traces while magic fixed their new shape. When I lifted it out, the edges were still straight. The circuit at its centre was quite different.

## Step 2

I held the first board up to the lamp for a good while. The traces ran from one end of the crystal to the other, and the edges had not chipped. I pressed a few more. Each circuit landed in the same place; the press was still intact.

Any of the old Inscriber presses can be reworked in the crucible, using equal measures of cognition, order and magic essentia. Once I have one, a block of iron in the Inscriber will give me a copy. That should save me a few evenings at the crucible.

I laid the finished boards beside a storage component and checked the connections. There is room, and the traces line up. Tonight I can try assembling a terminal.
