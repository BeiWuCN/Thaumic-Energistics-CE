# Wireless Golem Backpack

After collecting items, the golem always returns to a chest to unload. With the chest far away, it spends more time walking than collecting.

I fitted a wireless connection to a backpack, bound it to the network with a memory card, and gave it to the golem. When it became idle, the items appeared directly in the ME terminal.<PAGE>Transmission stopped beyond the wireless access point's range, and also when the network shut down. Its working area must remain within the signal.

Sneaking and right-clicking with a golem bell removes the pack. Right-clicking with a supported camouflage block changes its appearance. If the golem is to carry it all the time, it may as well match.
