# Thaumic Crystals

Thaumic Crystals are used in the mod's circuit boards, processors, and tools. The Thaumonomicon shows the infusion recipes for turning budding quartz into budding Thaumic Crystal blocks.

Leave space on the faces of a budding block so clusters can grow. An <ItemLink id="ae2:growth_accelerator" /> can speed up a budding Thaumic Crystal block beside it. Harvest the crystals and use them to make circuit boards or upgrade your equipment.
