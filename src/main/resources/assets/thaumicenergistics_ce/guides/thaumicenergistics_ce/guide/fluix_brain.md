# Forbidden Knowledge: Fluix Brain

## Step 1

Occasional insight is no longer enough. Fluix resonance and a thaumic processor can stabilize the signals inside a zombie brain, but its original structure cannot survive the procedure.

This forbidden research follows the Probability Box and incurs warp.

## Step 2

Infuse a zombie brain with fluix crystals, thaumic processors, an ender pearl and salis mundus. Use the resulting Fluix Brain on your Probability Box after installing a Brain in a Jar.

One Fluix Brain is consumed and one ordinary zombie brain is returned. Ownership and acceleration cards remain intact. Empty or already upgraded boxes consume nothing.<PAGE>Default success chances improve from 67% to 85% at 30 seconds and from 45% to 60% at 180 seconds, with linear interpolation. Server configuration may change these values. Existing rounds are not rerolled; the upgrade affects new rounds.

Removing the jar or breaking the box returns only an empty jar. The Fluix Brain is lost. A new ordinary brain jar restores ordinary odds.
