# Box of Probability

## Step 1

The brain in a jar responded to Cognitio essentia, but repeating a trial did not repeat its result. Sometimes a thought became clear; sometimes I waited a long while and the notes went no further.

I have connected a knowledge core to the box and will record the time and yield of each turn. Enough results should tell me how useful it really is.

## Step 2

The Probability Box is one complete block and needs no separate upper structure. Install a Brain in a Jar by using it on the placed box to bind its owner. Supply a channel, energy and Cognitio.

Each round costs 2 Cognitio and 500 AE, taking 30 to 180 seconds. Up to four acceleration cards each reduce the longest wait by 25 seconds, to 80 seconds.<PAGE>Ordinary brains default to a 67% success chance at 30 seconds and 45% at 180 seconds, with linear interpolation. Success grants 5 to 7 draws of 1 to 3 points, equally split between observation and theory. The following forbidden research unlocks a Fluix Brain; see its research for the irreversible removal cost.
