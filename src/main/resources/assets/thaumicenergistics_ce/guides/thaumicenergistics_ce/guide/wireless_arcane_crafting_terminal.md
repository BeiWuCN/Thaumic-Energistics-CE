# Wireless Arcane Crafting Terminal

## Step 1

Making a replacement arcane item in the field usually means a journey back. The wireless terminal can already retrieve the materials; it only lacks a workbench and somewhere for the wand.

I have reduced the crafting terminal's arrangement, keeping the grid and wand slot. The first trial will be the same recipe, completed over a wireless connection.

## Step 2

After linking the handheld terminal to a wireless access point, I could carry the ingredients, crystals and wand with me. They remain inside the handheld terminal when I close it.

Its upgrade cards stay with it too. A Vis Connection Card charges the wand from the bound relay output; crafting draws environmental vis from the chunk where I stand.

With no connection, I can still open the terminal, though no network stock appears. A Quantum Bridge Card and its paired singularity can reach a powered quantum bridge instead, without an access point binding.
